---

Programmable payment operations, governed by design. The Next PaymentOS MCP lets AI tools read balances, draft payouts, and submit work for review — through an allowlisted, scoped surface. Agents never release funds.

<Note>
  Sandbox only. Regulated outcomes remain **Partner review** or **Compliance review**, subject to jurisdiction. No real funds move.
</Note>
