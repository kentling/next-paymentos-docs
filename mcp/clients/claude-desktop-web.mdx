# Claude Desktop and Web

Next PaymentOS is available as a connector in Claude. The same steps work in Claude Desktop and on the web.

## Setup

1. Open the Next PaymentOS connector page on Claude, or in Claude go to **Customize > Connectors > Add > Browse connectors** and select **Next PaymentOS**.
2. Click **Connect**.
3. Sign in with your Next PaymentOS account to authorize access.

<Info>
On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, non-admins may need to request access before connecting.
</Info>

## Usage

Once connected, try:

> Search my USD balance and draft a payout to *[recipient]* for review.
